FATF Recommendation 16 — Wire Transfers: Financial institutions must ensure that wire transfers include full originator and beneficiary information. Any wire transfer above €1,000 must include sender name, account number, and address. Missing information is grounds for refusing or suspending the transaction.

EU 6th Anti-Money Laundering Directive (6AMLD): Extends criminal liability for money laundering to legal persons including companies. Introduces 22 predicate offences including cybercrime, tax crimes, and environmental crime. Member states must implement minimum 4-year imprisonment for money laundering convictions. Banks must report suspicious activity regardless of amount when intent to launder is suspected.

EBA Guidelines on Risk Factors: Banks must apply enhanced due diligence for customers who are politically exposed persons (PEPs), customers in high-risk third countries, customers with complex ownership structures, and customers whose transactions have no apparent economic purpose. Cash-intensive businesses require enhanced ongoing monitoring.

Danish FSA AML Rules: Cash transactions above DKK 50,000 (approximately €6,700) must be reported to the Danish Money Laundering Secretariat. Banks must file Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) within 2 business days of suspicion arising. Failure to report carries fines up to DKK 10 million for institutions.

EBA Guidelines on Correspondent Banking: Banks must not enter into or continue correspondent banking relationships with shell banks. Before establishing a relationship, banks must collect sufficient information about the respondent bank's AML controls and ownership structure. Wire transfers from unknown correspondent banks must be treated as high risk.

Nordic Financial Intelligence Units (FIU): Denmark, Sweden, Norway, and Finland all operate Financial Intelligence Units that receive SARs from financial institutions. Cross-border cooperation is mandatory under the Nordic AML cooperation framework. Suspicious transactions involving any Nordic country counterparty should be reported to the relevant national FIU.
